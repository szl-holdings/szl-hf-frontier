# Yarqa

Auditable compartmentalization for flow-derived models.

Yarqa converts eligible flow-field inputs into compartmental-network representations
with explicit provenance boundaries. Clean-room, Apache-2.0.

[GitHub source](https://github.com/szl-holdings/yarqa) |
[Evidence ledger](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SZLHOLDINGS/szl-lake) |
[Product](https://a-11-oy.com) |
[Proof](https://a11oy.net)

## What it does

1. Accept a declared flow or velocity-field input.
2. Partition it into an auditable compartment model.
3. Emit structured, provenance-oriented metadata.
4. Refuse to fabricate input evidence or physical validation.

This Space does not claim live instrumentation, validated CFD accuracy,
engineering certification, or safety-critical use.
