# Chaski-R2

## What this is

A separate research and proposal-only SKU based on `Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B`. The root [training_receipt.json](https://huggingface.co/SZLHOLDINGS/chaski-r2/blob/4ef29684c56de07626b1cefb4b93a1b486de2aa1/training_receipt.json) describes bf16 LoRA with r=16 and alpha=32. It does not overwrite [SZLHOLDINGS/chaski](https://huggingface.co/SZLHOLDINGS/chaski) and is separate from [SZLHOLDINGS/chaski-5050](https://huggingface.co/SZLHOLDINGS/chaski-5050).

## What the evidence does and does not establish

The **5/5 + 6/6 records are real published measured records**. Their public receipt files do not cryptographically bind those results to one exact public adapter-file SHA-256 or merged-weight revision. No third-party benchmark, broad capability estimate, or qualification follows.
