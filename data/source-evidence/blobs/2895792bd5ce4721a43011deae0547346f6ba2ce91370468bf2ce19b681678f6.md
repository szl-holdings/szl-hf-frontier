## How to read it

- **MEASURED** (bright teal) — the estate carries measured evidence: receipts, benches, signed runs
- **Declared** (blue) — a manifest fact from the consolidation receipt
- **BLOCKED** (dim) — a gate denied; the field shows the denial
- **INVALID / malformed** — renders *nothing*, and the HUD counts every fail-closed drop

Reporting glow where none was measured is forbidden by doctrine — visible in the engine's
admission gate, not just in prose.
