# Readiness Is Not Evidence

## Fail-Closed Epistemic Boundaries for Governed AI Services

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.21332338.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21332338)

This repository is the public landing page for a 24-page preprint defining fail-closed epistemic boundaries for governed AI services. It separates service readiness from evidence quality, requires explicit abstention and not-evaluated states, preserves provenance and uncertainty, and prevents operational health signals from being misreported as trustworthy knowledge.
